Jordan Lin

👋🏼  My name is lín林kuān寬héng恒. I go by Jordan and publish under Kuan Heng Lin.

I'm a first-year PhD student at Columbia University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Aleksander Hołyński and Micah Goldblum. I am also part of CAIL 🥬. I am interested in computer vision, multimodal generative models, and machine learning.

I was a Research Intern at Netflix Eyeline Labs, where I worked on 4D-grounded video generation and scene editing with Ning Yu. I was also a Research Engineering Intern at Snap Research, where I worked on 2D parallelism for scaling up video diffusion training (Snap Video V2) with Willi Menapace.

I did my undergrad at UCLA in Computer Science (+ Math minor), during which I had a great time working with Bolei Zhou and Sicheng Mo on controllable image generation.

I think pixels are cool, and I do 📷 film photography and used to do 🎥 filmmaking.
